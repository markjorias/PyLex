Lexical Element Dictionary

Keywords

"def" | "if" | "while" | "return"

Reserved words defining structural control flow.

Identifiers

[a-zA-Z_][a-zA-Z0-9_]*

User-defined labels for variables and functions.

Numbers

[0-9]+(\.[0-9]+)?

Integer and floating-point numeric literals.

Strings

\"([^\\\"]|\\.)*\"

Character sequences enclosed in quotes.

Operators

"+" | "-" | "*" | "/" | "=="

Symbols for mathematical and logical operations.

Punctuation

":" | "(" | ")" | "[" | "]"

Structural delimiters and separators.

Comments

#[^\n]*

Annotations ignored by the analyzer.

Errors

. (Catch-all)

Invalid characters flagged for manual review.

How it Works

01

Input Stream

The raw code is fed into the PyLex Engine as a continuous stream of characters.

02

Regex Matching

Flex utilizes finite state automata to match the longest possible patterns against predefined regex.

03

Token Stream

Successful matches are emitted as structured tokens (Type, Value, Line) for the backend.
